FAQ

Answers to common questions

Didn't find your answer? Contact us directly.

Ask us on WhatsApp

Do I need technical experience?

No. The interfaces are simple, and we set up the platform and train your team before launch.

Can I start with just one service?

Yes. Start with a website, store, booking or accounting, and add the rest later.

Can the store connect to inventory and accounting?

Yes. Orders update stock and create invoices automatically within the same platform.

Does it support Arabic and English?

Yes. Every interface is available in both languages, and your customers can choose.

Are invoices compliant with Oman VAT?

Yes. The system includes Oman VAT settings and issues tax invoices in Omani Rial.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Connections are encrypted, each business's data is fully separated, with staff permissions and regular backups.

Can I use a domain with my business name?

Yes. Your site can use a domain in your business name, and we help you register and connect it.

How does payment work?

A monthly subscription based on your plan. Plan details are coming soon.
